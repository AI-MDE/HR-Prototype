# Glossary

## Purpose

The "Glossary" rules of the business-requirements Target that no other Feature supplies.

## Impact on business-requirements

Cover domain nouns, roles, statuses, and key processes; leave out generic English and technical jargon. When a term is renamed in Specs, rename it in the glossary too.

## Audit

- **Glossary:** Is every domain term used in the Specs defined, with renames applied?
