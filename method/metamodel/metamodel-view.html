    domain

    Application purpose, scope, boundaries, and capability map.

    diagram

    Requirements-level Mermaid use-case and logical data model diagrams.

    capability

    A meaningful business ability or area of responsibility.

    entity

    A durable business concept with identity, relationships, or lifecycle.

    use-case

    An actor or trigger using the system to achieve a goal.

    workflow

    An end-to-end process spanning multiple interactions.

    business-rule

    A policy, derivation, eligibility rule, or durable constraint.

    role

    A business responsibility relevant to permissions or use cases.

    architecture

    A durable engineering decision with rationale and consequences.

    page

    A UI screen with presented information, actions, states, and navigation.

    api-contract

    An endpoint or operation contract with request, response, and errors.

    data-model

    The physical persistence realization of business entities.

    integration

    An external-system connection with data, timing, failure, and security rules.
