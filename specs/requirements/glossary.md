# HR-Prototype glossary

## Entries

- **Client** — The organization receiving work through one or more projects. [Concept](capabilities/project-assignments/client/client.md).
- **Employee** — A person employed by the organization; the primary entity of employee records. [Concept](capabilities/employee-records/employee/employee.md).
- **PerformanceReview** — A recorded assessment of one employee over a period, with strengths and development actions. [Concept](capabilities/performance-management/performance-review/performance-review.md).
- **Project** — A defined body of work for one client; owns its employee assignments. [Concept](capabilities/project-assignments/project/project.md).
- **ProjectAssignment** — One employee's responsibility and capacity commitment to one project for a period. [Concept](capabilities/project-assignments/project/project-assignment.md).
- **Allocation percentage** — Share of nominal working capacity committed on each inclusive assignment day; distinct from hours worked or staffing demand fulfilled.
